### 1.4 Computational Disclaimer (Indices & Machine Learning)
Crucial disclaimer clarifying the algorithmic nature of the platform's outputs to prevent false legal claims and set scientific expectations.

```markdown
[TECHNICAL DISCLAIMER] STATISTICAL & SPECTRAL LIMITATIONS

ATTENTION NODE OPERATORS: This dashboard displays machine-generated predictions and computational spectral indexes. Please observe the following technical parameters:

* NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index): 
  Measures chlorophyll density. Rapid decreases suggest canopy clearance, which frequently precedes or accompanies heavy machinery access and excavation.
* BSI (Bare Soil Index): 
  Highlights mineral and soil exposure. Sharp increases signal soil disturbance, topsoil stripping, or active excavation pits.
* MNDWI (Modified Normalized Difference Water Index): 
  Delineates surface water open boundaries. Shifts in water channels reveal morphological alterations from riverbed dredging and illegal dredging channels.
* YOLO PREDICTION ENGINE: 
  Our neural network scans spatial tiles for machinery, barges, sorting sieves, and stockpiles. A high confidence rating (e.g., 88% YOLO Match) denotes statistical visual resemblance, not definitive legal proof of illegal activity.

Atmospheric interference (cloud cover, dust), seasonal hydrology, and agricultural cycles can generate false positive anomalies. Always cross-reference multiple sensor passes and coordinate with local ground teams before filing official reports.
```
